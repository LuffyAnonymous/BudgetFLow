# Milestone 2: Transactions & Budgets

Milestone 2 introduced cash flow tracking and monthly plans.

## Completed Features
1. **Ledger Table**: Full search, category filter, transaction type filter, pagination, and modals for creating, updating, and deleting transactions.
2. **Category Budget Reconciliations**: Compiles planned vs actual values, computes percentages, and assigns track statuses.
3. **Daily Food Allowance**: Groups food categories using a unique `budgetGroupKey` and aggregates their spending.
4. **Timezone Math**: Implemented exact UTC ranges offset by +4 hours.
